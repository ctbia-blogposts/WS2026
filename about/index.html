About the seminar

Current Trends in Biomedical Image Analysis explores current research in biomedical image analysis and machine learning for medical data.

The seminar belongs to Machine Learning for Medical Data at Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf, in Winter Semester 2026/27.

Reading research. Building understanding.

Students engage with recent research papers, explain their central technical ideas, interpret the evidence, and discuss where the approaches may fail. The focus is on understanding methods and their implications for biomedical research.

An illustrated technical blog

An illustrated technical article is an alternative to the conventional seminar report. It combines academic rigor with visual explanation: background, diagrams, equations, results, and critical discussion should help a technically competent classmate understand the paper.

The proceedings are intended as a pedagogical resource for readers learning about these research areas. The six current topic pages are previews; they are not completed student submissions.
